# Environment lighting asset

Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal / Jarod Guest, Poly Haven.
Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky
License: CC0 https://polyhaven.com/license
1K HDR panorama. The current browser extracts cloud coverage/shading from this CC0 asset and suppresses the photographed solar hotspot. The visible sun and lighting follow the simulated solar position. The generated sky also supplies environment reflections. This is not a photograph of the estate.
